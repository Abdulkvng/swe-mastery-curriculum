# Quant Math Quiz

1. If X and Y are independent, E[X+Y] equals what?
2. Does linearity of expectation require independence?
3. Write variance as an expectation identity.
4. When does `Var(X+Y)=Var(X)+Var(Y)` hold?
5. State Bayes' rule.
6. What is covariance intuitively?
7. Why can correlation fail to imply causality?
8. What is look-ahead bias?
9. Why can random train/test splitting be wrong for time-series prediction?
10. What is overfitting?
11. Why can repeated hypothesis testing generate false discoveries?
12. Why are floating-point additions non-associative?
13. Why can a parallel reduction disagree with a serial one?
14. What is catastrophic cancellation?
15. What problem does Kahan-style compensated summation address?
16. A fair coin pays +$3 on heads and -$2 on tails. Expected payoff?
17. Probability at least one head in 3 fair flips?
18. Two independent events have P(A)=0.4, P(B)=0.5. P(A∩B)?
19. What does stationarity mean at a high level?
20. Why can statistically significant predictive correlation still fail as a trading strategy?

## Selected answers

16. `0.5*3 + 0.5*(-2) = $0.50`.
17. `1 - P(all tails) = 1 - 1/8 = 7/8`.
18. `0.4*0.5 = 0.20`.
20. Costs, latency, impact, instability, leakage, capacity, risk, nonstationarity, and weak out-of-sample effect can erase it.
